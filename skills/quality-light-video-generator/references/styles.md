# Video styles: what to offer, when, and how each is produced

Offer the 4 that fit the chosen idea. Formats that work well on Reels/TikTok right now are marked 🔥; if web search
is available and the owner asks what's trending this month, check before claiming anything specific, and never
quote performance numbers you don't have.

| Style | Best for | Duration | Owner provides | Produced by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **3D + motion graphics** (house style) | explainers, mistakes, specs, comparisons, before/after | 45–80 s (short cut 20–30 s) | the WAV from AI Studio | fully here: 3D renders + graphics + music (`production-3d.md`) |
| 🔥 **Satisfying switch-on** (ASMR click, layer by layer, no voice) | transformations: majlis, garden, façade, kitchen | 8–15 s | nothing | here: 3D states + click SFX + music, 1–3 words on screen |
| 🔥 **Quiz «A ولا B؟»** | engagement + teaching: CCT, beam angle, glare, spacing | 10–20 s | optional short WAV | here: 3D comparisons + countdown, answer in the caption or a follow-up |
| 🔥 **POV storytelling** | emotional hooks: first night in the new villa, guests arrive | 15–30 s | WAV (or text-only) | here (3D) or with AI clips |
| 🔥 **Hyperreal AI cinematic** | premium mood: luxury villa, café ambience, façade at night | 10–30 s | clips generated from our prompts (Veo, Kling, Sora, Runway…) + WAV | prompts here, edit here (`production-footage.md`) |
| 🔥 **UGC / talking head** | trust & sales: owner or staff in the showroom, product in hand | 20–45 s | phone footage (we give the script + shot list) | edit here (`production-footage.md`) |
| **Product hero** (macro, spinning fixture, light beam) | a single product: deep spot, track head, COB strip | 8–15 s | nothing (or product photos) | here: a 3D product body + graphics |
| **Showroom / behind the scenes** | visits: "come see the colour in front of your eyes" | 15–30 s | phone footage of the showroom | edit here |
| **Myth vs fact / listicle** | quick tips, 3–5 points | 20–40 s | WAV | here: 3D or pure motion graphics |
| **Green-screen explainer** | owner explains over a 3D render or before/after | 20–40 s | footage in front of a plain wall | edit here: footage cut out over renders when the background is plain, otherwise picture-in-picture |

## Durations by purpose
- 8–15 s: satisfying, product hero, quiz, ads for reach.
- 20–30 s: POV, single tip, AI cinematic mood piece, short version of an explainer.
- 45–80 s: full explainers with several points + offer + address (the house style; best for saves and shares).

## Notes per style
- **Satisfying**: no voice; music with a steady beat + a crisp click on every switch; hold the final "all on" frame 2 s;
  end on logo + «خلّ بيتك ينوّر صح». Caption carries the explanation.
- **Quiz**: show A and B (same room, one variable), a 3-2-1 countdown, ask «اكتب A ولا B في الكومنت»; reveal in the last
  2 s or keep it for a follow-up reel. Keep options fair: one variable only.
- **AI cinematic**: 4–6 shots of 3–5 s; keep the same room, materials and fixture count across shots; the prompts in
  `production-footage.md`. Remind the owner that generated clips must not contain text, logos or signage.
- **UGC**: natural, handheld but steady, good window light or the showroom lights; the speaker talks in his own dialect
  (an Egyptian speaker should not fake Kuwaiti; Kuwaiti staff can). Captions on UGC are optional: ask.
