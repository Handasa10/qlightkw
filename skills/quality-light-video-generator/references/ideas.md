# Ideas: ranking, what was already made, and where new angles come from

## Ranking (most to least important)
1. A clear problem or desire a buyer recognises in his own home or shop.
2. The difference can be **seen** in a short vertical video (before/after, A vs B, light switching on).
3. Fits Kuwait: majlis, diwaniya, villa finishing, gypsum ceilings, hot dusty outdoors, cafés and boutiques.
4. A realistic CTA: visit the showroom, WhatsApp, send the villa plan (design fee deducted on purchase).
5. Not a repeat of something already produced.

Content mix to aim for over a month: ~40% problems & fixes, ~30% products & comparisons, ~20% before/after &
transformations, ~10% direct selling (visit, offer, product of the week; only with confirmed facts).

## Angle generators (when the bank runs dry)
- Quiz: «أي صورة أحلى؟ A ولا B» (answers come in comments: good for the comment agent and reach).
- Guess the mistake; same lamp two beam angles; same room 2700K vs 4000K.
- POV: «POV: أول ليلة في بيتك الجديد», «POV: ضيوفك دخلوا المجلس».
- Myth vs fact; 3 questions before buying a dimmer / strip / track.
- Kuwaiti situations: summer heat & dust outdoors, Ramadan majlis, national-day façades, wedding season diwaniya.
- Series names (ask before making one official): «دقيقة نور», «إضاءة بيتك مع QL».
- Turn followers into visitors: «تعال شوف درجة اللون قدام عينك», «جيب صورة الديكور ونختار معاك».
