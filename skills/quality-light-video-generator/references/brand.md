## Technical facts to stay consistent with
- Colour temperature: 3000K warm for living rooms, majlis, bedrooms; 4000K for kitchens, bathrooms, work areas;
  don't mix temperatures in one space.
- CRI 90+ recommended (true colours of wood, fabrics, food, skin).
- Anti-glare / deep-recessed spots for comfort; beam 36° for general light, 24° or narrower for accent.
- Spot spacing roughly every 1–1.2 m depending on ceiling height; wall washing 50–60 cm from the wall.
- Levels (approx.): living/majlis 150–300 lux, kitchen 300–500, bathroom 200–300.
- IP44 minimum near water, IP65 in showers and outdoors; outdoor/garden fixtures IP65+.
- Quality differences: LED chip, driver (flicker-free), aluminium heat sink, warranty.
- Layering: general + hidden/cove + accent + decorative, each on a dimmer or scene.
- 24V strips run from a driver; never imply they connect straight to 230V.
